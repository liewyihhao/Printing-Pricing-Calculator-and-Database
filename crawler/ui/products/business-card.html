Artwork Specification

How to set up print-ready artwork for Business Card so it prints exactly as you expect.

File format & setup

File formatPrint-ready PDF preferred. AI, EPS, or high-resolution PNG/TIFF also accepted.
Resolution300 dpi at 100% size. Vector art (AI/EPS/PDF) is ideal and stays sharp.
Colour modeCMYK for accurate print colour (RGB is converted and can shift).
Bleed3 mm on every side — extend background artwork into the bleed.
Safe marginKeep text and logos ≥ 3–5 mm inside the trim so nothing is cut off.
FontsOutline or embed all fonts before exporting.

Card setup

  • Supply front and back as separate pages.
  • For rounded corners, keep content ≥ 4 mm inside the trim.
  • For spot UV or foil, supply a separate 100% black mask layer.

Before you send

  • Export at 1:1 scale (100%).
  • Double-check spelling, contact details and the final size.
  • Flatten transparencies, or supply layered files if we've asked for them.

Not sure? Send us your file and we'll check it before printing.
