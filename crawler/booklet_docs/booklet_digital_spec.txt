How to Calculate Process Days?
1. Process Day = Quantity Process Day + Finishing Process Day
2. When choosing more than 1 finishing, the finishing that require the most number of days, 
    will be the amount of days to add to delivery.

You order 5,000 books with Matt Lamination + Silkscreen Spot UV on Cover, UV Varnish on Content.
